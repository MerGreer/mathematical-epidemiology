Activity 14.2.

It sometimes appears that our models result in a larger number of Infectious people, or a larger number of New Cases, than we see in our datasets. One contributing reason to this can be that the dataset does not report all the people who have become infected with the disease. The two most common reasons are (1) asymptomatic illness, in which a person does not know they have the disease, and (2) underreporting, in which not all known cases are reported, which leads to them not appearing in the dataset. Related to these, some people experience mild symptoms but do not recognize them as being related to an illness such as influenza. This is yet another reason these cases may not be reported.
