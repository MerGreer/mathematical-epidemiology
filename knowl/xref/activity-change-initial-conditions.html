In the models of Chapter 10 we consistently started with 23 Infectious people and considered the rest of the population, 2911 people, to be Susceptible. What if we instead determine that some people may already be immune, and therefore they begin in the Removed compartment? Or what if some people begin in the Exposed-and-incubating compartment rather than the Susceptible compartment because we find it likely that they have already begun to incubate the disease when the first 23 cases appear?
