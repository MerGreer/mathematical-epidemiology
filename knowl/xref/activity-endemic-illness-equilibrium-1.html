In this activity, we solve algebraically for the endemic equilibrium of the compartmental model shown in Figure 12.3. The differential equations for this model are:
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{dS}{dt} \amp = \alpha R - \beta SI\\ \frac{dI}{dt} \amp = \beta SI - \gamma I\\ \frac{dR}{dt} \amp = \gamma I - \alpha R\text{.} \end{align*}
🔗
