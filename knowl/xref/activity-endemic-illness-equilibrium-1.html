Activity 12.6.

In this activity, we solve algebraically for the endemic equilibrium of the compartmental model shown in Figure 12.3. At equilibrium, all three differential equations from the model in Activity 12.6 are set equal to 0, indicating that the values are unchanging for all three populations \(S(t)\text{,}\) \(I(t)\text{,}\) and \(R(t)\text{.}\) Follow the steps below to determine the combination of algebraic formulas for which this is true.
🔗
\begin{align} \alpha R - \beta SI \amp = 0 \tag{12.2}\\ \beta SI - \gamma I \amp = 0 \tag{12.3}\\ \gamma I - \alpha R \amp = 0 \tag{12.4} \end{align}
🔗
  1. Solve the \(dI/dt=0\) equation for \(S\text{.}\) In other words, rearrange \(\beta SI - \gamma I=0\) into an equation beginning with “\(S=\)” and having only non-\(S\) terms on the right-hand side.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Solve the \(dS/dt=0\) equation for \(I\) and replace \(R\) with \(N-S-I\text{.}\) Explain why this is a permissible substitution. Then group together the \(I\) terms and solve for \(I\text{.}\) Your answer will be in terms of \(S\text{,}\) which we solved for in part (1).
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Use the \(dR/dt=0\) equation, that is, \(\gamma I - \alpha R=0\) to solve for \(R\text{.}\) Your answer will be in terms of \(I\text{,}\) which we solved for in part (2).
    🔗
